# Starting a field notebook

*Sample entry — replace this with your own words before publishing.*

The idea is simple: record a little of what happened, keep the useful parts, and return to the work.

## A small session log

- **Tried:** A game, a Linux tool, or a small piece of code.
- **Noticed:** One interesting detail or something that did not work.
- **Next:** One thing worth trying another day.

A screenshot, a short clip, and a few sentences can be a complete entry. A video can stay on YouTube, linked from the note, while the longer recording stays on the SD card or backup drive.

The [project shelf](../projects/index.md) holds work that spans more than one session.
